### TEMPLATE: proof_ordering_gap (AUTHORITATIVE)

You must implement the Designer plan as a **short proof/argument ordering or missing-step** question.

#### Required stem structure
Choose ONE:

A) Missing justification:
- Provide a short proof skeleton with a labelled step that says “Therefore …”
- Ask which justification is valid for that step (or which missing statement fills the gap).

B) Ordering:
- Provide 4–6 statements (steps) labelled A–F (not I/II/III) and ask which ordering forms a valid argument.

#### Option form
- For missing justification: options are candidate justifications (A–F).
- For ordering: options are candidate orderings (keep short; 4 steps max) or “which step must come next”.

#### Distractor requirements
Wrong options should be typical invalid moves:
- assuming converse/implication reversal
- using examples as proof
- circular reasoning
- hidden division by zero / domain errors
- invalid algebraic manipulation

#### Constraint
Keep it within Paper 2 expectations: no long proofs, no advanced theorems.
