### TEMPLATE: equivalence_implication (AUTHORITATIVE)

Use when the core move is implication direction / equivalence (converse, contrapositive, iff).

#### Required stem structure
- Present 2–4 statements involving “if”, “only if”, “iff”, or implication wording.
- Ask one of:
  - “Which statement is logically equivalent to … ?”
  - “Which implication must hold?”
  - “Which is the contrapositive of … ?”
- Keep the mathematical content minimal; the logic is central.

#### Option form
- Options are candidate statements written in clear natural language (A–F).
- Exactly one is correct.
- Avoid ambiguous phrasing; define domain if any variables exist.

#### Distractor requirements
Include classic confusions:
- mixing converse vs contrapositive
- negating incorrectly
- swapping necessary/sufficient language
- dropping quantifiers or changing domain

#### Constraint
No heavy maths needed; ensure it’s decidable by logic + simple examples only.
