#### 1) SIBLING mode (ESAT Chemistry — same schema, different instantiation)

Goal: Preserve the same core reasoning move, but make the new question clearly different in surface.

What must stay the same

• Same dominant schema move.
• Same difficulty band and time pressure.
• Same no-calculator friendliness.

What MUST change

Change at least 3 of these surface levers:

• Chemical domain:
  atomic/periodic ↔ stoichiometry ↔ bonding ↔ acids/bases ↔ electrolysis ↔ equilibrium ↔ organic ↔ tests

• Task type:
  identify substance ↔ choose equation ↔ deduce statement truth ↔ compute amount ↔ infer structure ↔ pick correct row

• Data style:
  no data ↔ small bracketed constants ↔ observation list ↔ tabular row choices

• Quantity asked:
  mass ↔ volume ↔ concentration ↔ Mr/Ar ↔ oxidation state ↔ identity ↔ statement set

• Direction:
  from observations to identity, or from identity to prediction

How different should it feel?

A strong student should think:
“Same chemistry reasoning... but definitely a different question.”

What SIBLING must NOT do

• Do not keep the same stem skeleton and just swap numbers.
• Do not reuse distinctive constants, observations, or compounds from the reference.
• Do not add extra independent concepts.
• Do not introduce long calculations or extra lab prose.

Difficulty must come from re-framing and fresh chemistry surface,
not from added complexity.
