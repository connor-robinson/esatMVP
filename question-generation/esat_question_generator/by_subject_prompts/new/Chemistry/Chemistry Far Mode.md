#### 2) FAR mode (ESAT Chemistry — strongly remixed but still natural)

Goal: Keep the same schema invariant, but make the question feel meaningfully different while remaining authentic ESAT Chemistry.

What must stay the same

• Same schema invariant.
• Same difficulty band.
• Still short, school-chemistry, and no-calculator.

How to make it feel different

Use one or two remix operators:

1) Change representation
   - direct quantity ↔ statement-combination
   - equation choice ↔ observation deduction
   - identity question ↔ row/table question

2) Change chemical setting
   - same reasoning move in a different topic area
   - e.g. proportional reasoning in gases, titration, electrolysis, or combustion

3) Hide the deciding condition
   - provide observations or supplied data that imply the key relation indirectly

4) Swap the ask
   - instead of finding a value, ask which option could be correct or which statement must be true

What FAR must NOT do

• No off-spec chemistry.
• No puzzle-style gimmicks.
• No long chains of chemical facts.
• No long calculations.
• No dependence on special practical knowledge beyond spec.

Calibration rule

FAR should feel fresh, but once the key chemistry relation is seen,
the solution must collapse quickly to standard school-chemistry reasoning.

What to output in Designer JSON (FAR only)

surface_twist:
1–2 sentences describing what makes the surface feel different.

why_still_on_spec:
1–2 sentences naming the chemistry domains used and explaining
why the structure reduces to standard ESAT Chemistry moves.
