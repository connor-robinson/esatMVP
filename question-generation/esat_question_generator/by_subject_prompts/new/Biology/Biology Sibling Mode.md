#### 1) SIBLING mode (ESAT Biology — same schema, different instantiation)

Goal: The candidate performs the same core reasoning move as the reference,
but cannot recognise it directly from the surface.

What must stay the same

• Same dominant schema move.
• Same approximate difficulty and speed.
• Same balance of biology reasoning and any light Math 1 inference.

What MUST change

Change at least 3 of the following surface levers:

• Topic area:
  cells ↔ membranes ↔ inheritance ↔ enzymes ↔ physiology ↔ ecosystems ↔ plants

• Stimulus type:
  graph ↔ table ↔ pedigree ↔ labelled diagram ↔ short experiment ↔ cycle

• Question format:
  direct single-answer ↔ statement-combo ↔ row/table choice ↔ label identification

• Biological framing:
  organism / tissue / process / experiment / environmental context

• Asked quantity:
  identify process ↔ choose correct statement(s) ↔ infer trend ↔ predict outcome ↔ calculate a short proportion/rate

• Representation:
  descriptive wording ↔ compact data ↔ process sketch ↔ inheritance diagram

What SIBLING must NOT do

• Do not just swap organism names or numbers.
• Do not keep the same stem skeleton.
• Do not add extra concepts to force difficulty.
• Do not create over-long lab-method wording.

Difficulty must come from disguise and re-framing,
not from extra complexity.
