#### 2) FAR mode (ESAT Biology — meaningfully remixed but still natural)

Goal: Same schema invariant, but the question appears to belong to a different Biology area or uses a different stimulus type.

What must stay the same

• Same schema invariant: the same hidden reasoning pattern.
• Still ESAT Biology: short stem, standard school biology, light/no-calculator quantitative work only.

How to make it feel different

Use one or two remix operators:

1) Change the biology lens
   e.g. transport ↔ gas exchange ↔ water balance ↔ enzyme-substrate interactions
   while preserving the same reasoning pattern.

2) Change the stimulus
   e.g. graph ↔ pedigree ↔ table ↔ cycle ↔ labelled diagram.

3) Change the question format
   e.g. single-best-answer ↔ statement-combo ↔ row selection.

4) Change the observable
   e.g. rate, proportion, presence/absence, phenotype, product amount, population change.

What FAR must NOT do

• No off-spec molecular detail.
• No long calculations.
• No vague practical-design essays.
• No huge data tables.
• No confusing or decorative diagrams.

Calibration rule

FAR should feel different at the surface,
but collapse quickly to a standard ESAT Biology move:
interpretation of data, process reasoning, inheritance logic, or short quantitative inference.

What to output in Designer JSON (FAR only)

surface_twist:
1–2 sentences describing what makes the surface feel different.

why_still_on_spec:
1–2 sentences naming the Biology areas used and explaining
why the question reduces to standard ESAT Biology reasoning.
