P15|This pack contains two original Physics modules. Each candidate paper has 27 multiple-choice questions for a 40-minute, no-calculator sitting. One mark is available for each correct answer and there is no penalty for a wrong answer.
P16|Editorial calibration
P17|The drafting benchmark was the current ESAT Physics specification and official example guidance, cross-checked against official ENGAA and NSAA Section 1 papers from 2019, 2020, 2022 and 2023. The recurring target was: short wording, one decisive conceptual step, limited arithmetic, and distractors tied to specific mistakes.
